# BRANDING.md

Visual and verbal identity for Daily Warrior's Lesson and The Forge. Copied here because the README cites it; the longer family document also lives in `menearz/forged-path-app`.

## One-line promise

Forge good men into better men — one hard lesson at a time.

## Color

- Background: near-black `#0B0D10`
- Surface: `#1B2028`
- Primary text: `#E7E9EC`
- Secondary text: `#9AA3AD`
- Accent: ember orange `#FF6A1A` or blood red `#B22222`

## Imagery

- Lesson images: wide landscape paintings, statues, battle scenes, or architecture. Show the complete subject.
- Never crop a portrait into a thin banner.
- Never use coins, plates, medallions, or close-up details as covers.

## Voice

- Short sentences. Concrete nouns.
- No corporate filler: no "unlock," "level up," "gamify," "crush it."
- Prefer "forge," "discipline," "endure," "stand," "build," "lead."
- End pages with: "When the game is over, the king and the pawn go back into the same box."

## Naming

- Product lineage: **Daily Warrior's Lesson**
- Intake: **The Forge**
- Paid step: applying the aimed 70 / 30 feed after The Forge
