Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Daily Warrior's Lesson is a reading app. It shows one lesson a day and an archive of previous lessons.

What we collect

We do not create user accounts. We do not sell data. We do not run ads. The app does not require a login.

On your device, the browser or installed app may store a local cache so pages load faster. That cache stays on your phone.

Images and third parties

Some lesson images are loaded from Wikimedia Commons. Those servers may see a normal image request from your device, the same way any website image request works.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on github.com/menearz/daily-warrior-lesson.
