Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Daily Warrior's Lesson is a reading app. It shows one lesson a day and an archive of previous lessons. The Forge is an optional paid path inside the same site.

This site is built and maintained with AI assistance.

What we collect

We do not create user accounts. We do not sell data. We do not run ads. The app does not require a login.

On your device, the browser may store a local cache, your Forge answers (`forged_path`), and a paid-return flag (`forge_paid`) so the aimed feed can open after checkout. That data stays on your phone.

Payments

Applying the aimed daily feed is a $12 USD one-time payment through a Stripe Payment Link. Stripe processes the card. This site never sees your card number. After you pay, Stripe returns you here with a success query parameter so the browser can mark the path as paid.

Images and third parties

Some lesson images are loaded from Wikimedia Commons. Those servers may see a normal image request from your device, the same way any website image request works. Checkout happens on Stripe's site.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on github.com/menearz/daily-warrior-lesson.
