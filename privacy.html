Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Daily Warrior's Lesson is a reading app. It shows one lesson a day and an archive of previous lessons. The Forge is an optional paid path inside the iOS and Android apps.

This site and the store apps are built and maintained with AI assistance.

What we collect

We do not create user accounts. We do not sell data. We do not run ads. The app does not require a login.

On your device, the browser or installed app may store a local cache, your Forge answers (forged_path), and an entitlement flag (forge_unlock_entitled) after a store purchase. That data stays on your phone.

Payments

In the iOS and Android apps, applying the aimed daily feed is a one-time in-app purchase (product ID forge_unlock, suggested $12 USD). Apple or Google process the payment. The app never sees your card number. Restore Purchase re-reads the store account.

The website is not the paid channel. The free daily lesson does not require a purchase. Do not use Stripe inside the iOS app for this unlock — Apple will reject it.

Images and third parties

Some lesson images are loaded from Wikimedia Commons. Those servers may see a normal image request from your device, the same way any website image request works.
