# Daily Warrior's Lesson

A Progressive Web App that delivers one verifiable lesson each morning — from the Stoics, Robert Greene's full shelf, Homer, Socrates, Alexander, Washington, the Crusades, and Scripture.

## Why a PWA and not a store app

No App Store or Play Store review, no fees, no signing keys. It installs on your home screen like a real app, opens full-screen, and updates itself every morning.

## The image problem, solved

Notion crops cover banners to a fixed thin strip — you only ever saw a slice. This app does not. The lesson image is rendered at full width with its natural height, so the **entire picture** is always visible. No crop, no banner, no repositioning.

## Install

1. Open https://menearz.github.io/daily-warrior-lesson/ on your phone (or any browser).
2. iPhone: tap the Share icon, then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Android: tap the menu, then **Install app** / **Add to Home screen**.
4. It now lives on your home screen like any other app.

## How it updates

The Daily Warrior's Lesson automation rewrites `index.html` each morning at 7 Central with that day's lesson, quote, and a wide landscape image. Open the app and you see today's briefing — image fully visible.
